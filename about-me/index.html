About Me

Daniel Pimentel is an Associate Professor of Immersive Media Psychology and Director of the University of Oregon’s Reality Lab.

His research focuses on the prosocial and pro-environmental uses and effects of augmented and virtual reality technology.

Download CV My research Get in touch

Bio

His award-winning immersive storytelling projects have been supported by organizations such as National Geographic, Unity 3D, Snapchat, Meta, The Sea Turtle Conservancy, and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA).

Research testing the effects of his experiences have also been published in peer-reviewed academic journals such as Nature Scientific Reports, the Journal of Nature Conservation, and New Media & Society, among others.

He is currently a Visiting Professor at OceanX, an official Specs Ambassador, and has previously served as a Fellow at Yale’s Program on Climate Change Communication.
